# Leetmate: Your Coding Accountability Partner

**Stop the grind. Start the game.** Leetmate is a browser extension that turns technical interview preparation into an engaging RPG adventure.

## Why Leetmate?
Practicing algorithms can be lonely and repetitive. Leetmate provides the psychological "hook" of a virtual pet to keep you coming back every day until you land that dream offer.

## Product Features
- **🐾 Virtual Pet Companion**: Your progress is their survival. Solve problems to keep your pet happy and healthy.
- **🔥 Streak System**: Build unstoppable momentum. Use "Streak Freezes" to protect your progress during busy days.
- **📊 Level Up Your Career**: Gain XP with every solved challenge. Watch your level rise as your coding skills sharpen.
- **💰 In-Game Economy**: Earn coins for your hard work and spend them in the Store to customize your experience.


## Vision
To bridge the gap between "knowing you should practice" and "actually wanting to practice." Leetmate is more than a tool; it's a companion for your professional growth.
